RL Uploader

RL Uploader is a personal tool that records Rocket League replays and uploads the resulting gameplay videos to its owner's own YouTube channel, using the YouTube Data API.

It is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts or services to anyone else, and it only accesses the YouTube channel of the person who signs in to it.
